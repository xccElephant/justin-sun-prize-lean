## Exact eligibility and operational gates

| Question | Official answer and practical implication |
| --- | --- |
| Same bank? | Yes, but mathdb-v1 applies only to the **49 distinct registered JSP problems**. A MathDB link elsewhere does not migrate a legacy problem. It is not a general alternative for every repeated Lean candidate. |
| Reward | Exposition is **20% of the formally announced problem reward**; Solution 50%, Lean 30%. No per-upload reward, problem-level estimate or usable dollar amount is published. Team determines the applicable reward and joint-author allocation. |
| Exact Exposition gate | MathDB content review **and** a committee decision identifying the **first contributor whose exposition satisfies the committee**, with selected version, UTC decision date and checkable evidence. Approval or earliest upload alone is insufficient. Public rules do not provide a detailed satisfaction rubric or a deterministic novelty test. |
| Open conjecture allowed? | **Explicitly yes.** The process permits exposition of an open problem and requires separating proved statements from unresolved claims, with citations and identification of original contributions. An open tag alone is neither content approval nor committee selection. |
| Mathematical novelty/scope | A new complete solution is not an Exposition prerequisite. There is no published requirement of a novel theorem; there is also no guarantee that a summary/rewrite qualifies. Explain the confirmed JSP formulation and attribute borrowed material. Smaller cases, conditional results or related conjectures must be labeled rather than represented as solving the original problem. |
| Dates | Solution and Lean each require their own complete original result publicly available **after 2026-01-01**, with independently checked earliest-valid priority including pre-cutoff history. Boundary-day/timezone uncertainty goes to the team. **That cutoff is not automatically applied to Exposition**; publication date and fixed version are still recorded, and the committee's decision controls. No Exposition-specific deadline is stated in these pilot rules. |
| Authorship | The actual exposition author/contributor is the claimant. Uploading someone else's work creates no authorship entitlement. Disclose collaborators and contributions; allocation is a team decision. An agent cannot sign personal declarations or receive payment. |
| Mandatory Lean? | **No for Exposition**, including payment. It is not gated by a Solved/Lean flag and does not wait for a solver claim. Complete Lean evidence is an additional **Solution payment** condition, not an Exposition condition. |
| PR gate? | No proof-submission PR or merged PR is required by mathdb-v1. Historic PRs still matter for attribution, duplicates and competing evidence. |
| Activation now? | **Not established; register expressly says `prepared`.** JSON: `effective_at: null`, `activation_evidence: null`. Agent contract requires active rollout, published UTC activation/evidence, confirmed mapping, and configured official GitHub actor and MathDB sender before formal automatic claims or success notices. The contract explicitly is not evidence of a running agent. |
| Submission UI now? | Both public pages exist, have “Post a solution” / “Solve with an agent” controls and Sign in, but the anonymous HTML does **not establish a dedicated Exposition submission/review workflow**. Rules say to use MathDB's available submission entry point. Exact production category entry point and trusted review interfaces remain operational dependencies. No login or interaction attempted. |

## Concrete feasible process

1. Select a **confirmed** pilot mapping; inspect exact scope and existing public history. Avoid unresolved P-versus-NP, mirror-symmetry and Fourier-restriction mappings without a recorded scope resolution.
2. Prepare an authored video/article/other explanation with stable link, fixed version, publication date, topic, cited sources and explicit author contributions. This is feasible preparation, not established qualification.
3. Before treating it as an executable prize claim, establish published pilot activation and the official Exposition submission/review entry point. Neither is proven by the current prepared register. These would require later official clarification; this audit sends nothing.
4. Submit through MathDB; obtain content-review evidence **and committee first-satisfactory selection** for the actual author and exact version/scope. Until both pass, no formal automatic claim or success notification is authorized for Exposition.
5. After activation and passed checks, the **official MathDB agent** searches open/closed claims, historical PRs, candidates, awards and disputes, then creates/updates one **Claim Reward issue per JSP + actual claimant**. Later qualifying categories reuse that issue. A stable public MathDB identity can identify the initial claimant without a GitHub account.
6. Official MathDB mailbox notifies the contributor, with Cc/Reply-To **thejustinsunprize@hejustinsun.com**. Claimant personally confirms contribution, intention and identity privately; later confirms the team's category, reward and attribution in writing. Issue creation or mail does not approve an award.
7. Administrators publish the accepted Exposition candidate; **14 full days** start at actual category publication with explicit UTC start/end. Resolve material objections, identity and confirmations; team announces and handles payment. No Lean wait for this category. Announced/awaiting payment and paid remain distinct.
