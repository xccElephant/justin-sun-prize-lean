## Exact scope and proof path

**699 / 843.** For every two-coloring of the positive square values, there is a cutoff N such that every n ≥ N is a sum of a finite set of distinct positive squares all having one color. N is quantified after the coloring; the chosen color may vary with n. The actual definition uses `Finset ℕ` of square **values**, with `IsPositiveSquare q := ∃ m, 0 < m ∧ q = m^2`. It does not claim one fixed color represents the whole tail or require arbitrary numbers of colors. CFP Theorem 1.2 gives an r-Ramsey-complete subsequence of every complete polynomial sequence; P(m)=m² and r=2 gives the catalog question.

The existing final body supplies concrete indexed square blocks, `ramseySquareBlock_robust`, and `ramseyBlockIntervals_overlappingUnbounded` to `squaresRamseyTwoComplete_of_robust_blocks`. The robustness and overlapping/unbounded interval premises have actual proofs in the same file, rather than being additional hypotheses on `erdos_843`. The finite interval construction and its imported growth/Lev support are present. No final connector is missing.

**298 / 360.** The original domain is **{1,…,n−1}**, not all positive integers including n (the singleton {n} would make avoidance impossible). f(n) is the minimum number of classes avoiding a monochromatic sum of distinct elements equal to n. The complete answer is

`f(n) ≍ n^(1/3) · (n/φ(n)) / ((log n)^(1/3) · (log log n)^(2/3))`.

The exact endpoint asserts positive absolute constants c,C and both inequalities eventually for all natural n. `BelowTarget`, `AvoidsTarget`, and the `Nat.find` definition of f implement the genuine minimization; `resolutionScale` implements the explicit CFP formula, including the totient and both logarithms. It is not a tautological definition of an answer. Small n and log singularities are excluded by the eventual quantifier, as appropriate for an order-of-growth question.

The final body obtains `exists_eventually_controlledPrimeRandomTheorem`, then applies `resolution_of_controlledPrimeRandom` with the proved `cfpLevHighMultiplicityPrinciple`. The connector in `Erdos360/PrimeRandomControlledEventually.lean:353` assembles the lower and upper bounds. These inputs have proof bodies in the pinned tree; the final statement adds no unresolved source assumption.
