# Certificate attribution

The CNF and LRAT files are unmodified mathematical certificate data by Cecil Jentges,
from the exact b5738d8944340f0ac9434f70f689316b18497026 source linked in RESULTS.json.
They are supplied here for reproducible verification, not claimed as our proof data.
The source repository now explicitly licenses manuscript material CC-BY-4.0 and
code Apache-2.0 at 9ef9e5670ee322365c3adbe04e7ca4579ee21dab.
The local replay wrappers use Mathlib's kernel-producing lrat_proof command.
Their success proves the indicated Boolean formulas are unsatisfiable, without
certifying correspondence to arbitrary integer subsets.
